# Reply guidelines

The drafting prompt in `app/drafting.py` follows Google's own guidance on replying to reviews and common local SEO practice.

## Principles

- Short, polite and conversational. No promotions or offers.
- Personal: reviewer first name, one specific detail from the review, signed by the business.
- Same language as the review. Google translations are stripped before drafting.
- Never disclose private information or argue in public.
- Admit real mistakes, apologize when warranted, move complex issues offline.
- Written for future customers reading the profile, not only the reviewer.

## Keywords

Replies may mention a service or the city once, only when natural. Google has not confirmed that replies affect ranking. The keywords in the reviews themselves matter more, so ask customers to mention the service and location when requesting reviews.

## Length by rating

| Rating | Reply |
| --- | --- |
| 5 and 4 | 1 to 3 sentences: thanks, a detail, invitation to return |
| 3 | 2 to 4 sentences: thanks, acknowledge the issue |
| 2 and 1 | 3 to 5 sentences: acknowledge, apologize if warranted, offer contact |
| No text | One short thank-you |
